The Program

The Good Measure Foundation

A six-week life-readiness program for young men aged 14–21 who need confidence, responsibility, discipline, and direction.

Structure

Six weeks. One standard. Visible progress.

The program combines parent intake, young man assessment, weekly mentoring sessions, practical challenges, and a final 30-day action plan.

Each week focuses on one area of growth and ends with one clear commitment.

Week 1 – Starting Point

Assessment, trust building, parent concerns, and personal snapshot.

Week 2 – Confidence

Strengths, self-image, social confidence, and one uncomfortable action.

Week 3 – Standards

Responsibility, discipline, habits, screen use, effort, and personal expectations.

Week 4 – Direction

Work, trades, college, university, business, interests, and next options.

Week 5 – Readiness

Communication, resume, money basics, professionalism, and adult conversations.

Week 6 – Next Measure

Progress review, 30-day action plan, and parent summary.

Included

What families receive

Parent Intake

A clear understanding of the concern before the program begins.

Self-Assessment

A starting score across confidence, habits, responsibility, direction, and readiness.

Workbook

Weekly exercises, challenges, reflections, and action planning.

Final Summary

Wins, growth areas, and the recommended next 30 days.

The Good Measure Program

A six-week life-readiness experience for young men aged 14–21.

Week 1 – Starting Point

Assessment, trust building, and personal snapshot.

Week 2 – Confidence

Strengths, self-image, and difficult conversations.

Week 3 – Standards

Responsibility, discipline, and personal expectations.

Week 4 – Direction

Work, trades, college, university, and future planning.

Week 5 – Readiness

Communication, resumes, money, and professionalism.

Week 6 – Next Measure

30-day action plan and parent summary.

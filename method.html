The Method

Measure. Mark. Move. Maintain.

A practical framework for helping young men turn conversation into action, and action into responsibility.

The Four Movements

Measure

We establish the starting point: confidence, responsibility, habits, communication, school or work effort, and future direction.

Mark

We identify the one area that matters most right now. Not everything at once. One clear target.

Move

We assign a practical weekly action. Something the young man can actually do, prove, and report back on.

Maintain

We review progress, adjust the standard, and build consistency over time.

The Standard

Good Measure is built around observable behaviour.

Confidence matters, but it has to show up somewhere. Responsibility matters, but it has to become action. Direction matters, but it has to turn into decisions.

The method focuses on what can be seen, practiced, and improved.

The method is the product.

It can be used in one-on-one mentorship, group cohorts, school workshops, parent programs, and future facilitator training.

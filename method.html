The Method

Measure. Mark. Move. Maintain.

A repeatable framework for turning conversation into action, and action into responsibility.

Four MovementsObservable BehaviourFive MeasuresApply

Framework

The four movements

Measure

Establish the starting point: confidence, habits, responsibility, communication, school or work effort, and future direction.

Mark

Identify the one area that matters most right now. Not everything at once. One clear target.

Move

Assign a practical weekly action that can be completed, proven, and discussed.

Maintain

Review progress, adjust the standard, and build consistency over time.

The Standard

Built around observable behaviour.

Confidence matters, but it has to show up somewhere. Responsibility matters, but it has to become action. Direction matters, but it has to turn into decisions.

The method focuses on what can be seen, practiced, and improved.

The Five Measures

What the method develops

Self

Confidence, identity, strengths, and values.

Standards

Responsibility, discipline, effort, and habits.

Skills

Communication, time management, money, and work readiness.

Direction

School, work, trades, college, goals, and future planning.

Action

Weekly commitments that turn ideas into movement.

The method is the product.

Good Measure can be delivered one-on-one, in groups, through workshops, and eventually by trained facilitators.

Start with a parent call.

Good Measure begins with a clear conversation: what is going on, what has already been tried, and whether the program is the right fit.

Application Flow

Selective, structured, and parent-led.

This is not a generic sign-up form. The first step is a parent intake so the situation is understood before a young man enters the program.

Parents should be ready to explain the concern, the goal, the current school or work situation, and what success would look like after six weeks.

What We Ask

Application questions

Parent Details

Name, email, phone, location, and preferred contact time.

Young Man

Age, grade, school or work status, interests, and current responsibilities.

Main Concern

Confidence, motivation, responsibility, communication, direction, school effort, or work readiness.

Success

What would make the next six weeks feel worthwhile?

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Good Measure for?

Young men aged 14–21.

Is this therapy?

No. Good Measure is a life-readiness and mentorship system.

How long is the program?

The core program is six weeks.

Can parents participate?

Yes. The process begins with a parent intake and ends with a parent summary.

Can schools use the program?

Future versions are being developed for workshops and partner organizations.

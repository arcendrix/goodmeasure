The Good Measure Method

Measure. Mark. Move. Maintain.

A practical mentorship system built around weekly action, honest conversation, and steady progress.

The Why

Teens don’t need another lecture.

They need someone steady enough to listen, honest enough to challenge them, and experienced enough to help them see what they are capable of becoming.

Good Measure exists because growing up is harder when you have no direction. We help teens build confidence, character, responsibility, and real-world readiness through practical mentorship and coaching.

The System

The Good Measure Method

A repeatable framework for helping teens move from drifting to direction.

01

Measure

Understand where the teen is now: confidence, habits, school effort, communication, responsibility, and future direction.

02

Mark

Choose what needs to improve first. One problem. One goal. One behaviour to work on this week.

03

Move

Turn the conversation into action with a practical weekly challenge and a clear commitment.

04

Maintain

Review progress, build accountability, and create the next small step forward.

6-Week Program

A clear starting point for teens who need direction.

The Good Measure Program combines parent intake, weekly 1:1 mentorship sessions, practical challenges, and a final parent summary.

Week 1

The Starting Point

Build trust, understand the teen, and complete a personal snapshot.

Week 2

Confidence

Identify strengths, self-image, and what real confidence looks like.

Week 3

Standards

Set expectations around habits, effort, phone use, school, and responsibility.

Week 4

Direction

Explore future options: work, trades, college, university, business, and interests.

Week 5

Readiness

Build practical life skills: resume, job readiness, money basics, and communication.

Week 6

The Next Measure

Create a 30-day action plan and final parent summary.

For Parents

For the parent who knows their teen is capable — but stuck.

This program is for families dealing with low motivation, lack of confidence, poor follow-through, school pressure, screen habits, unclear direction, or the transition toward adulthood.

  • Parent intake before the program begins
  • Weekly focus and action steps
  • Clear boundaries and professional communication
  • Final summary with next steps

What Good Measure is not

Good Measure is not therapy, counselling, tutoring, diagnosis, or mental health treatment. It is mentorship, coaching, accountability, and life direction.

When a teen needs clinical support, families should contact a qualified mental health professional, physician, school support team, or emergency services if urgent.

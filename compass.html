How to read it

The score points to the work.

Below 45

Build structure first. The starting point may need clearer routines, standards, and adult support.

45–69

Develop consistency. The foundation is there, but follow-through and direction may need support.

70+

Strengthen leadership. Focus on contribution, responsibility, and next-level independence.

The score is not a label. It is a starting point.

The Compass helps parents and young men see where structure, support, and direction may help most.
